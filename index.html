Before You Hire Another Marketing Agency

Most businesses start by hiring a digital marketing agency first. Sometimes that works. But plenty of owners discover months later that the real issue wasn't the website, the ads, or the SEO. It was the underlying strategy — or the complete absence of one.

Before you spend $10K, $20K, or more on marketing execution, we identify the constraint that actually needs fixing. Then you can move forward with confidence — whether that means hiring me, hiring an agency, or implementing the plan yourself.

Situations I See All the Time

A successful service firm running almost entirely on referrals — grateful for every one of them, but with zero intentional lead generation underneath it. A website redesign that looked gorgeous but didn't produce a single extra client. Social media posting that generates likes and comments and activity — but not revenue. Because likes ain't cash. Strong expertise paired with weak online authority, so prospects comparison-shop and end up hiring someone louder. Marketing investments that feel expensive and never quite produce the return that justified them.

In almost every case, the issue isn't effort. It's that the real constraint was never identified — so the effort went in the wrong direction.

Why Hasn't Anyone Told You This?

Because the sexy stuff gets all the attention. Hustle culture says "just start" without mentioning the unsexy infrastructure that actually works. Influencers push whatever pays them. And platforms want you posting, not optimizing — because your posting is their product.

And because you're running a business, not studying digital marketing. Nor should you be. You understand your craft. My job is making sure the market understands it too.

What's the Solution?

The exact fix depends on your situation. Sometimes the fix is a smarter website. Sometimes it's clearer messaging. Sometimes it's SEO, or better follow-up systems. Sometimes the smartest move is killing a tactic that was never going to work for you in the first place.

But here's the rule I never break:

No tactics without strategy.

Tactics only work when they're pointed at the real problem. And most people skip the strategy part — not because they're careless, but because nobody told them it was an option.

When we work together, nothing gets recommended until we've found what's really blocking your growth right now. Maybe it's your website. Maybe it's not. Maybe you need SEO. Maybe you need to stop funding it. Maybe you should post more. Maybe you should post way less and fix the follow-up that's quietly losing you leads.

When you fix the right thing, everything else starts working better with less effort.

That's the difference between strategy and tactics. Tactics are what you do. Strategy is knowing which tactics to do, when, and why.
